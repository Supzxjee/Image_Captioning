# Diagnose supervision before scaling caption training

Completed local work: inspect selected claims and exact subject/object boxes on three purposively chosen development images (089033,130024,348636). Seven claims include two supported visible relations, four visibly unsupported relations and one uncertain target localization. These are assistant visual assessments, not independent human truth, not training labels and not a representative precision estimate.

Examples: foreground person carrying bananas is assigned riding a background bicycle and eating bananas; child swinging a bat is assigned holding an airborne ball; skier is assigned holding a ski at the feet. On the ball image, the wrong holding-ball score .895899 exceeds the supported holding-bat score .673530. On the banana image, cross-predicate score-minus-threshold priority puts riding above holding. The score/margin is not calibrated correctness. This is evidence that merely amplifying relation influence can amplify errors. Default captions largely avoid these actions despite the prompts.

The existing pilot trained against caption CE only, using old heuristic action relations. It neither supplies instance-discrimination supervision nor requires every selected relation to appear in the caption. Failure to mention a relation is not necessarily failure: reading a book may entail holding it, without using the word holding. Do not automatically add such a requirement or convert unmentioned relations into negatives.

The CPU-only audit measures lexical verb/object cooccurrence in the actual first-five reference captions for the same512 training images, and in references/candidates for the12 inspected evaluation images. It checks byte hashes against the returned pilot manifest. It does not parse subject/object identities or establish semantic entailment; synonym gaps, negation and multiple actors can invalidate a literal match. Two unit tests explicitly preserve this distinction. No calibrated reranker or new loss is implemented by this audit.
